Projects

AI Supplier-Sourcing Agent

Designed and built an AI procurement agent for submarine electronic warfare systems that turns a 100+ part list into a ranked supplier shortlist. The agent researches candidate suppliers for every component, scores them against user-defined priorities, and delivers an Excel workbook comparing lead time, price, stock, and other key details, automating what would otherwise be a lengthy manual sourcing effort. I owned the project end to end, applying requirements-engineering and software design principles to author the documentation that serves as the agent’s knowledge base.
